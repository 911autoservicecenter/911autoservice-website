Brake repair in Coldwater

Squeaks, a soft pedal, pulling to one side, or a brake warning light — we diagnose and repair brakes on cars and light trucks at 22 S Clay St.

We service pads, rotors, calipers, brake hoses, and ABS concerns. If the pedal feels wrong or the truck pulls when you stop, do not wait it out on Michigan roads — bring it in.

Shop hours are Mon–Thu, 8am–5pm. Call 517-677-3173 or pick a time online. If you cannot drive it safely, call towing at 517-279-2010.
