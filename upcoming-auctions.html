When we schedule a vehicle auction, the date and vehicles will appear here — each listing includes photos, year, make, model, and VIN. Call ahead with questions.

How auctions work

  • Check this page for the next scheduled auction and the vehicles on the list.
  • Cash only — no exceptions. Bring cash to pay for any vehicle you buy. We do not accept cards, checks, or other payment methods.
  • Valid operator’s license required — no exceptions. You must present a valid driver’s license (operator’s license) to bid or purchase.
  • Purchased vehicles must be removed at the end of the auction. Plan to take your vehicle with you when the sale ends.
  • No vehicle may be driven off our lot without proper plates and insurance.
  • Need a tow? We have tow trucks available for hire to move vehicles for you. Call 517-279-2010 to arrange.
  • Vehicles left more than 24 hours after the close of an auction will begin accruing storage fees.
  • Call 517-677-3173 (auto repair line, Mon-Thu 8am-5pm) with questions about a vehicle or the sale.
  • Auctions are held at 22 S Clay St, Coldwater, MI unless noted otherwise.
