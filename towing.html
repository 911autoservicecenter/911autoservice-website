Towing & roadside — we go anywhere, any time

Branch County’s primary AAA towing provider for close to 50 years. Our team brings over 100 years of combined experience — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. When you want the best, you know who to call.

24/7 emergencies — call 517-279-2010

When you call, press 1 for towing and impounds or press 2 for auto repair. This number is for emergencies only — you are stranded, you need a wrecker, or you need impound help. Do not use it for estimates, appointments, parts, billing, or other shop business. Those go to the shop line at 517-677-3173 (Mon–Thu, 8am–5pm) or schedule online.

If you call this number outside normal business hours (Mon–Thu, 8am–5pm), you reach a dispatcher. Dispatchers do not know what vehicles are in our lot, and they cannot access bills or fees. For those questions, call the office during shop hours at 517-677-3173.

What to call us for

  • Tows and recoveries

    Breakdowns, wrecks, ditch work, and flatbed tows when the vehicle should not roll. Tell us where you are and whether the vehicle can roll, steer, or start.

  • I-69 and highway stops

    We regularly run I-69 through Branch County. If you are on the shoulder, stay in a safe place, share the nearest mile marker or exit, and we will come to you.

  • Lockouts and jump starts

    Roadside lockouts and dead batteries. If you need a key cut or programmed, that is shop work through our KeyWorks partnership — call the shop at 517-677-3173 during shop hours, or start with the tow line after hours.

  • Impounds

    If we already have your vehicle, see impound pickup requirements before you drive in. If you call this number outside normal business hours, you reach a dispatcher who cannot look up lot inventory, bills, or fees. Those are office questions during shop hours.

Where we go

We cover Coldwater and the rest of Branch County, including Bronson, Quincy, and Union City, plus nearby stretches of I-69. If you are not sure we can reach you, call anyway — we will tell you straight.

Shop repairs are a different line: 517-677-3173, Mon–Thu 8am–5pm (press 2 if you reach the 24/7 menu). You can also schedule shop work online. Do not call the 24/7 number for quotes or other routine business.

22 S Clay St, Coldwater, MI 49036 — we dispatch from here unless we agree otherwise when you call.
