Battery, alternator, and starting problems

If it clicks, cranks slow, or dies after a jump, we test the battery, starter, and charging system so you are not replacing parts you do not need.

We diagnose no-starts, parasitic drains, and charging faults. If you are stranded, call towing at 517-279-2010 (24/7). Shop testing and replacement is 517-677-3173, Mon–Thu 8am–5pm.
