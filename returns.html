Returns and exchanges

Contact the shop and get an RMA number before you send anything back. We do not issue a refund without an RMA.

Policy details

Effective date: November 10, 2026

This policy covers shop orders from 911 Towing & Repair, whether the product is shipped to you or installed at the shop at 22 S Clay St, Coldwater, MI 49036.

Ask for an RMA first

A return or an exchange starts when you contact us and we give you an RMA number. Call 517-279-2010 and press 2, Monday through Thursday, 8am to 5pm, or email sales@911autoservice.org. Include your name, the order number, the item, and whether you want a refund or an exchange.

Asking for an RMA does not mean one will be issued. We decide whether to issue it. Do not ship a product back, and do not remove an installed part, until we give you an RMA number and tell you what to do next.

No refund without an RMA

We do not issue a refund unless we have given you an RMA number. That is not negotiable. A phone call, an email, or a message by itself is not an RMA. If we did not issue an RMA, the order is not refunded.

Exchanges

An exchange needs an RMA too. If we approve one, we tell you what the item can be exchanged for. We do not exchange an item that was never approved.

Installed at the shop

If we installed the part on your vehicle, call the shop. Leave the part on the vehicle until we approve an RMA. Taking it off and mailing it in does not create a refund.

Shipping

The advertised price does not include shipping. If we issue an RMA for an item that was shipped, we tell you where to send it and how return shipping is handled.
