Check-engine light diagnosis

A check-engine light is a stored fault, not a guess. We scan it, test what it points to, and tell you what actually needs to be fixed.

We handle drivability problems, misfires, fuel and ignition faults, and related engine performance work. We are a Jasper Certified Authorized shop when an engine replacement is the honest repair.

Call the shop at 517-279-2010 (Mon–Thu, 8am–5pm) or schedule a diagnostic. Bring the vehicle to 22 S Clay St, Coldwater.
